## 📌 1. Process Engineering & Micro-Logistics Feeding Infrastructure

Based on the highly continuous cleanroom layout configured for multi-stage secondary purification, the cluster operates via two parallel, synchronized high-efficiency tracks:

### A. Non-Contaminating Inclined Belt Conveyors & Gravity Hoppers
* **Operational Mechanics:** Refined, high-purity quartz crystals are extracted from the primary stage and loaded onto dual inclined conveyor belts configured with non-reactive white synthetic treads.
* **Material Dispensing:** The crystalline aggregate is discharged into elevated stainless steel gravity-feed hoppers, creating a uniform, regulated particle curtain before entering the localized sensor-enclosure bays.

### B. Isolated Aluminum-Glass Sensor Enclosures
* **Structural Engineering:** The sorting bays are enclosed within heavy-duty anodized aluminum profile frames lined with high-transparency protective glass panels. This design acts as a strict airtight barrier, preventing ambient dust contamination or human interference from disturbing the falling grain trajectories.

---

## 🔬 2. Double-Spectrum Laser Refining & Rejection (TOMRA PRO Tech)

### A. Dual Aligned TOMRA PRO Secondary Sorter Modules
* **Cyber-Physical Sorting Dynamics:** The material passes through two fully autonomous **TOMRA PRO (Secondary Sorter)** systems operating in parallel. As the crystal matrix falls, dual synchronized laser scanning nodes emit high-intensity, cross-cutting neon-green and deep-blue monochromatic laser beams.
* **Spectrometric Micro-Purification:** The inline sensors evaluate light diffraction, atomic refraction indices, and chromatic deviations at the micro-second level. Integrated high-speed pneumatic pulse-valves fire micro-jets of compressed air to instantaneously eject any remaining sub-micron impurities down into separate waste hoppers, while the flawless 4N+ pure crystal grains drop directly into industrial stainless steel collection drums.

### أ. السيور المائلة المانعة للتلوث وأقماع التغذية بالجاذبية
* **آلية العمل التشغيلية:** يتم سحب بلورات الكوارتز المنقاة من المرحلة الأولية ونقلها عبر سيور ناقلة مائلة مزدوجة مجهزة بأحزمة بيضاء صناعية غير تفاعلية لمنع أي تلوث احتكاكي.

### ب. مقصورات الفحص المعزولة من الألومنيوم والزجاج المقوى
