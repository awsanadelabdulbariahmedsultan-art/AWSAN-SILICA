### A. Pressurized Aeration & Flotation Columns

* **Separation Dynamics:** High-volume bottom-up air diffusers introduce microscopic bubbles, creating intense effervescence. Selected collectors cause non-magnetic heavy mineral oxides to rise as dense, white upper froth for automated laundering, leaving pure silica at the base.
